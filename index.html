Puzzle contests nobody can rig. Not even the house.

The answer is sealed on Solana before the clue drops, and the purse is locked in a program escrow. Your guess is sealed to your own wallet, so nobody can copy it off the chain. When the house reveals, the program checks it, finds the earliest correct wallet and pays it. No judge to trust.

1 · Commit

The house posts sha256(id|salt|answer) to the contest inbox. From that slot on, the answer is fixed.

2 · Submit

You post sha256(id|wallet|guess). A correct guess looks like noise until the reveal and only counts for the wallet that sent it.

3 · Reveal & payout

The program refuses any reveal that doesn't match the seal, keeps the earliest correct wallet, and pays it the purse from escrow. No judge, and no house hand on the money.
